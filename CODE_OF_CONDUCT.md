# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct is taken from the Conduct of Use set out in the
[Mochi Terms of Use](https://github.com/T1nkiePlayz/Mochi-Website/blob/main/public/terms/index.html)
(effective 6 October 2026), operated under the name Side Quest Games in Western Australia.
It applies to everyone who uses Mochi, the Mochi website, the launcher, account services, this
repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions.

> In short: Mochi is a launcher and library-management tool. It does not provide games, does not
> host pirated content, and does not grant you rights to software you launch through it.

## Your games and third-party content

- You are responsible for the games, applications, mods, files, launch targets and other content
  that you add to or launch through Mochi. You must have the legal right or appropriate permission
  to possess and use that content.
- Mochi does not provide, host, upload, distribute, endorse or facilitate the distribution of
  pirated games, cracked software, unauthorised copies, stolen content or other unlawful content.
  Do not use Mochi, or this project's issue tracker and discussions, to infringe copyright or other
  intellectual-property rights. Requests for piracy help are removed.
- Mochi can launch user-selected executables, scripts, desktop entries, Flatpak applications and
  other targets. Side Quest Games cannot control or verify every program a user chooses to run.

## Third-party services

Mochi may integrate with services such as Google, GitHub, IGDB, SteamGridDB, Modrinth, Flatpak and
game platforms. They are independent from Side Quest Games and have their own terms, licences and
privacy policies. You are responsible for complying with them.

## Accounts and security

- You are responsible for the security of your account and for activity performed through it.
- Do not share credentials or authentication codes unnecessarily.
- If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us as soon as reasonably possible.
- Never post API keys, tokens, passwords or private environment files in issues or pull requests.

## Acceptable use

You must not use Mochi to knowingly:

- facilitate unlawful activity;
- distribute malware;
- infringe intellectual-property rights;
- bypass another service's access controls;
- abuse account or cloud systems;
- interfere with the operation of Mochi; or
- access another user's data without authorisation.

## Community behaviour

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, spam and
deliberately misleading reports are not tolerated. Assume good faith, keep feedback about the work
and not the person, and help newcomers.

## Enforcement

We may restrict, suspend or terminate access, and may remove comments, issues, pull requests or
contributors, where reasonably necessary to protect the service and community, investigate abuse,
comply with law, or address serious or repeated violations of this Code.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to **support@ashtontink.com**. Reports are handled in confidence.
Security vulnerabilities should be reported the same way instead of in a public issue.

This document may be updated as Mochi and its legal terms change. The current Terms of Use on the
Mochi Website are authoritative.
